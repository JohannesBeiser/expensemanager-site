Personal-Finances

Expenses, trips and income — on your phone, and nowhere else.

Most expense trackers convert everything at today's rate. This one doesn't. Every record keeps what you typed and the rates in force the month you typed it, so a total from 2021 still reads what it cost. Nothing is re-priced behind your back.

Coming to the App Store

What it holds

Expenses
Categories and tags, in whatever currency you paid in. Recurring bills keep a price history, so registering a rent increase does not restate every past month.
Trips
Trips nest — a city inside a country — and each one knows its own span, its total and what it cost per day, so a long trip and a short one can actually be compared.
Income
Payslips sit beside outgoings, so earned, spent and what is left are finally in the same place instead of two apps that never met.
Investing
Money paid in against what it is worth now, month by month, each month priced at the rate that applied then rather than at today's.

It stays on your phone

No account. No server. No subscription. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind. The whole ledger is a single file you can export whenever you like and read in a text editor. The only thing the app ever fetches is a share price on the investment screen — it sends a ticker symbol and receives a number — and nothing else depends on it.

The privacy policy says the same at greater length, including the one place data is visible outside the app. Support answers the questions this app actually produces.
