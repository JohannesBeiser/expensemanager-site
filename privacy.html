Privacy

Personal-Finances — a personal ledger for expenses, trips and income

Last updated 16 September 2026

This app collects nothing. There is no account to create, no server to sign in to, and no analytics of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

Your expenses, trips, tags, categories, income, investments and settings are held in a single file inside the app's own storage on your phone. Nobody else can read it — not the developer, not anyone. It is included in your device backup if you back your phone up, and it is removed when you delete the app.

You can export that file to yourself at any time from Settings → Backup & data. It is plain, readable JSON. Where you put it after that is your business, and it never passes through anything belonging to this app.

What leaves your phone

One thing, and only if you use the investment screen. To show what a holding is worth, the app requests a current share price and an exchange rate from public financial data services — currently Yahoo Finance, Stooq and Frankfurter. Those requests contain a ticker symbol and nothing else: no identifier, no account, no device information beyond what any web request unavoidably carries, and nothing about your spending, income or holdings. If the request fails, the app simply shows the last price it recorded.

If you never open the investment screen, the app makes no network requests at all.

The one place data is visible outside the app

So that you can find an expense from your home screen, the app adds your expense names, amounts, dates, categories and tags to your device's own Spotlight search index. This is a feature of iOS, it happens entirely on your device, and that information does not leave your phone or reach the developer. It is mentioned here because it is the only case where anything you have entered is readable outside the app itself.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage measurement
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking, and nothing shared with data brokers or any third party
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No location access, no contacts, no photos, no health data

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export or delete. Your own data is in your hands throughout: export it whenever you like, and delete all of it either from Settings → Backup & data or by deleting the app.

Changes

If the app ever starts doing something this page does not describe — syncing across your devices through iCloud, for example — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
