Common questions

Where is my data kept? Can I get it out?

On your phone, in one file, and yes. Settings → Backup & data → Export a backup writes readable JSON you can keep anywhere. The same screen imports it back. There is no account and no server, so an export is the only copy that exists outside the app — worth taking one before you change phones.

Why doesn't my total change when I switch between CHF and EUR at today's rate?

Because it shouldn't. Every expense stores the exchange rates that applied in the month you entered it, and converts at those. A total from 2021 is therefore not the same total divided by today's rate — the two differ across years of different monthly rates, and that difference is the correct answer rather than a bug.

I entered something in a currency the app doesn't know

For currencies without bundled rate history the app asks you for the rate once, on the record itself, and stores it there. You can correct it later by opening the expense. One rate is enough for both display currencies.

My rent went up. Will that change last year's totals?

No. A recurring bill holds a list of prices rather than one amount. Edit it and choose this month and future months, and every earlier month keeps what it actually cost. Choose this month only for a one-off.

An expense is on the wrong trip, or has no trip

The date decides the trip: change the date and the trip follows, unless you picked one by hand. A trip that has finished still shows in the picker marked (past), so an expense filed under it is never invisible.

The investment figure doesn't match my bank

It won't, and nothing is wrong. The app converts at the mid-market rate; a bank converts at its own rate with a spread, typically a little under one percent. The footnote under the chart prints the rate used. Prices also only accumulate on days you open the app.

I deleted something by mistake

Deleting an expense offers Undo for a few seconds. Past that, the way back is a backup — which is the argument for exporting one occasionally.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not yet. Today the ledger lives on one phone, and moving it means exporting and importing.

Reporting something wrong

If a number looks incorrect, that is worth an email. Include the screen, the month, what it shows and what you expected. If you are willing to send an exported backup it makes the cause much easier to find — but it contains your whole financial history, so only do that if you are comfortable with it, and never post one publicly.
